SIM & Mobile Guides

Official SIM verification and network help

Check SIMs on your CNIC

Use PTA's official SIM Information System to check the number of SIM connections registered against your own CNIC. Pak Point does not collect or store your CNIC.

Open PTA SIM Information System

Verify your own SIM

Send MNP to 667 from the SIM in your possession to use the operator's SIM verification process. Standard SMS charges may apply.

For SIM count by CNIC, send your 13-digit CNIC without dashes to 668. Use only your own CNIC and follow current operator/PTA instructions.
